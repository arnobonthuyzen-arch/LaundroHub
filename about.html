Our story

From one store at The Towers to laundry across Mangaung.

Laundro-Hub opened in August 2023 at The Towers in Langenhovenpark, with one goal: to make laundry the easy part of the week for the people of Bloemfontein.

What started as a drop-off store now handles everything from one family's weekly wash to daily linen for guesthouses, student homes and school hostels — 7 days a week.

With Laundro-Hub Express we collect and deliver across the Mangaung area, and we collect from Woodland Hills every day. Through it all, our motto hasn't changed: we care for the clothes you wear.
