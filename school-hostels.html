How it works

How hostel batch runs work.

  1. 1

    Schedule your batch days

    We agree on weekly collection and return days that align with school timetables and sports fixtures.

  2. 2

    Bags collected at the hostel

    Our branded van collects tagged laundry bags directly from the hostel storeroom, reception, or commune gate.

  3. 3

    Washed, pressed & packed

    Each learner's bag is washed separately, dried, steam-pressed, and neatly folded back into their designated bag.

  4. 4

    Prompt return & clear invoicing

    Returned fresh within 24–48 hours. Flexible term invoicing directly to the school or individual parent statements.
